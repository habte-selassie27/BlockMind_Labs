Blockmind Wallet

A GIWA-native wallet that shows you what you are signing.

Non-custodial by design: your keys are generated on your device, encrypted, and never sent anywhere. Every transaction is simulated and screened before you approve it.

Open the chat app

Install from source

  1. Build the extension bundle:
pnpm install --filter @blockmind/wallet-extension
pnpm --filter=@blockmind/wallet-extension build
  1. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  2. Choose Load unpacked and select apps/wallet-extension/dist.
  3. Reload the chat app — the Blockmind Wallet option will connect instead of reporting that the extension is missing.

What it protects you from

Silent reverts

Simulation runs before you are asked to approve, and a failing transaction is never signed.

Unlimited approvals

MAX_UINT256 allowances are blocked unless you explicitly opt in and acknowledge the risk.

Unknown contracts

Scam Shield screens each new contract; high-risk targets are refused outright.

Blind signing

Signatures and transactions are decoded into plain language, and opaque payloads require acknowledgement.

Network

GIWA Sepolia (chain ID 91342) and GIWA Mainnet (chain ID 9134). Gas is cheap and blocks confirm in about a second.

Keys never leave your device. Blockmind cannot recover your funds if you lose your recovery phrase — write it down and keep it offline.
